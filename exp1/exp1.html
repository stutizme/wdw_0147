B.Tech. Electrical and Computer Engineering

Welcome to the Course Curriculum of Electrical and Computer Engineering. This program combines electrical engineering with computer and programming concepts.

Program Highlights

The course focuses on technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, programming, electronics, and modern engineering applications. © 2026

Semester 1 Subjects

  1. Engineering Mathematics - I
  2. Engineering Physics
  3. Programming in C++
  4. Basic Electrical Engineering
  5. Engineering Graphics

Core Skills

  • Programming
  • Problem Solving
  • Electrical Circuits
  • Computer Fundamentals

Course Description

Programming
Learning programming concepts, algorithms and problem solving.
Electrical Engineering
Study of electrical circuits, systems and fundamental principles.
Computer Engineering
Understanding computers, software and computational techniques.

Learning Areas

  • Electrical
    1. Circuit Theory

    3. Power Systems
  • Computer Science
    1. Programming
    2. Data Structures
    3. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Career Opportunities

After completing the course, students can explore careers in software development, electrical engineering, artificial intelligence, embedded systems, and other technical fields.

Note: Students are encouraged to develop both technical and problem-solving skills.
